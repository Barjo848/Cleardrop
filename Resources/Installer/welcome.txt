This installs Cleardrop @VERSION@.

Cleardrop removes metadata from a PDF and saves a new copy. It works offline, has no account and collects no telemetry. Your original files are never changed.

It removes structural metadata such as the author, producer and file ID. It does not make a document anonymous if the text or images on its pages identify you.

The installer adds one item: the Cleardrop app. To remove it later, open Cleardrop and choose Cleardrop → Uninstall Cleardrop…
